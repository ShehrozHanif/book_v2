# Module 5: Applications, Ethics, and Future Directions

This module brings together practical applications, ethical considerations, emerging technologies, and provides guidance for your first robotics project.

## Chapters in This Module

1. **Chapter 17: Debugging & Troubleshooting** - Learn systematic approaches to diagnose and fix common robotics issues
2. **Chapter 18: Real-World Applications** - Explore real-world examples and use cases of humanoid robots in industry and research
3. **Chapter 19: Ethical Considerations** - Understand the ethical implications of humanoid robotics technology
4. **Chapter 20: Emerging Technologies** - Discover cutting-edge developments shaping the future of humanoid robotics
5. **Chapter 21: Competition & Benchmarks** - Learn how robots are evaluated, compared, and benchmarked
6. **Chapter 22: Getting Started: Your First Project** - Build your first humanoid robot project with step-by-step guidance

## Learning Path

This final module synthesizes knowledge from previous modules and prepares you for real-world robotics development.

[Start Module 5 →](/book/docs/module-05/chapter-17)

## Prerequisites

- Complete all previous modules (1-4) recommended
- Understanding of fundamental concepts from Modules 1-3 essential
- Familiarity with ROS2 and development tools

## Outcomes

After completing this module, you will:
- Know how to debug and troubleshoot robot systems effectively
- Understand real-world applications of humanoid robots
- Recognize ethical considerations in robotics development
- Be aware of emerging technologies and future trends
- Know how robots are benchmarked and evaluated
- Have built your own complete robotics project
- Be prepared to continue learning and developing in robotics
