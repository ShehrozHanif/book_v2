# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to your comprehensive guide to understanding and building humanoid robots. This textbook combines theoretical foundations with practical implementations to help you master the exciting field of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.

## What You'll Learn

This textbook takes you on a journey from fundamental concepts to advanced applications in humanoid robotics. Whether you're a student, researcher, or industry professional, you'll gain:

- **Solid Foundations**: Core concepts in kinematics, dynamics, and robot perception
- **Practical Skills**: Hands-on experience with ROS2, simulation, and control systems
- **Advanced Techniques**: Motion planning, whole-body control, and learning-based methods
- **Real-World Applications**: Industry use cases and emerging technologies
- **Ethical Awareness**: Understanding the societal impact of humanoid robots

## Course Structure

### 22 Chapters Across 5 Comprehensive Modules

#### Module 1: Foundations (5 Chapters)
Build your understanding of humanoid robotics from the ground up. Learn what makes a robot "humanoid," master the mathematics of motion, and understand how robots perceive their world.

**Topics**: Introduction to Humanoid Robotics, Kinematics Basics, Dynamics & Motion, Sensors & Perception, Hardware Overview

[Explore Module 1 →](/book/docs/module-01)

#### Module 2: ROS2 & Development (5 Chapters)
Dive into the Robot Operating System 2 (ROS2), the industry-standard framework for robot software development. Learn to describe, simulate, and control robots.

**Topics**: ROS2 Fundamentals, Robot Description (URDF), Simulation Environments, Motion Planning, Control Systems

[Explore Module 2 →](/book/docs/module-02)

#### Module 3: Advanced Kinematics & Motion (5 Chapters)
Master complex movement patterns and real-time control. From walking and balance to manipulation and whole-body coordination.

**Topics**: Real-Time Considerations, Advanced Kinematics, Walking & Locomotion, Manipulation & Grasping, Whole-Body Control

[Explore Module 3 →](/book/docs/module-03)

#### Module 4: Learning & Control (1 Chapter)
Explore cutting-edge learning-based control methods that enable robots to adapt and improve through experience.

**Topics**: Learning-Based Control

[Explore Module 4 →](/book/docs/module-04)

#### Module 5: Applications & Future (6 Chapters)
Look beyond the basics to see how humanoid robots are being applied in the real world and what the future holds.

**Topics**: Debugging & Troubleshooting, Applications, Ethics in Robotics, Emerging Technologies, Benchmarks, Getting Started (Your First Project)

[Explore Module 5 →](/book/docs/module-05)

## Prerequisites

To get the most from this textbook, you should have:

- **Basic Mathematics**: Linear algebra, calculus, and basic physics
- **Programming Skills**: Python experience recommended (we'll use Python extensively)
- **Computer Science Fundamentals**: Understanding of algorithms and data structures is helpful
- **Curiosity**: A genuine interest in robotics and AI

Don't worry if you're not an expert in all these areas - we'll introduce concepts gradually and provide resources to help you along the way.
